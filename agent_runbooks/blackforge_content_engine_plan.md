# Blackforge Workshop — Social Content Engine (Dash-run)

**Brand emotion:** Fan identity & fandom. We sell *who you are*, not what it's made of.
**Structure:** fandom is the loud front door; home decor is the everyday revenue behind it.
**Human input required:** John captures raw photo + video in the shop and drops it in a folder.
Everything else — editing, writing, scheduling, tracking — is Dash.

---

## The 4 content pillars

| Pillar | Share | What it is | Sells the feeling of |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Game Day / Fan Identity** | ~40% | Team coolers, custom fan gear, seasonal football tie-ins, "show your colors" | Belonging, pride, rivalry |
| **Watch It Made** | ~25% | Prints running, the peel, the reveal, satisfying timelapse | Craft, realness, "these are actually made" |
| **Made for Your Space** | ~25% | Lamps / planters / vases / statues styled in real rooms | An intentional, put-together home |
| **Behind Blackforge** | ~10% | The shop, John's story, customer features (UGC), Chicago angle | Trust, connection, local pride |

---

## Platforms (priority order)

1. **Instagram** — primary. Reels (process + fan) and carousels (decor styling). Everything starts here.
2. **Pinterest** — the sleeper. Home-decor pins are evergreen and drive *search* for years; huge for lamps/planters/vases. Low effort, long payoff. Pin every decor + finished-product shot.
3. **TikTok** — repurpose IG process/fan reels. Zero net-new work.
4. **Facebook** — Chicago local + fan community + shop updates. Cross-post fan-identity content; lean into local groups.

## Weekly cadence — 4 posts (sustainable)

| Day | Pillar | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Made for Your Space | Carousel or styled photo → also a Pin |
| Wed | Watch It Made | Reel (timelapse / reveal) |
| Fri | Game Day / Fan Identity | Reel or bold photo — heaviest push in football season |
| Sun *(flex)* | Behind Blackforge / UGC | Story-first, customer repost, or shop moment |

Plus low-effort **Stories** most days (polls, "which colorway?", behind-the-scenes) and
**Pins** batched weekly. Stories and Pins don't need to be produced — they're offcuts of the main 4.

## Caption system (the fan voice)

**Formula:** Hook (identity/emotion) → the object → one spec for the brain → CTA.

**Hook templates — fandom:**
- "You don't just watch the game — you [live it / bleed the colors / own Sundays]."
- "Every [team] fan needs one thing on their bar cart this season."
- "Show me your colors. Tell me your team in the comments 👇"
- "This is the [gift / desk piece / centerpiece] that says exactly whose side you're on."

**Hook templates — decor:**
- "Your [desk / shelf / entryway] called. It wants to look intentional."
- "The lamp that makes people ask where you got it."
- "Small change, whole different room."

**CTA rotation:** "Link in bio," "Comment your team," "Save this for game day," "Tag someone who needs this."

**Rules:** first line is the hook (it's all most people see). One clear CTA. 3–5 relevant hashtags,
not 30. Mention the product plainly once — never lead with specs.

## Dash production workflow

**Step 0 — John captures (his only job).**
Shoot raw photo + video in the shop — prints running, reveals, finished pieces, fan gear on the bar.
Drop everything into a dated folder:
```
C:\BlackforgeContent\_inbox\YYYY-MM-DD\
```
No editing, no naming. Just capture and dump.

**Step 1 — Dash produces (weekly batch).**
- Pull the week's raw clips from the inbox.
- Cut 3–4 short reels / select best stills. Timelapse the print footage; keep reels 7–15s.
- Write captions with the formula above, matched to the pillar for that day.
- Add hashtags + a Pinterest description (keyword-rich for decor items).
- Queue everything in the content calendar (below) with date, pillar, platform, caption, asset.

**Step 2 — Approve, then autopilot.**
For the **first two weeks**, Dash queues drafts and John green-lights before anything posts —
so the voice gets dialed in. After that, John can let Dash schedule and post on its own,
spot-checking weekly. (Posting to public social is John's call to hand off; default to
draft-and-approve until he says go.)

**Step 3 — Track.**
Weekly, Dash logs per post: **saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks** (not vanity likes —
saves/shares signal real intent). Flag the top performer; make more of what wins.

---

## Content calendar (Dash maintains — CSV or sheet)

Columns: `date | pillar | platform | format | hook | caption | hashtags | asset_file | status | saves | shares | link_clicks`

Dash fills a row per planned post, moves `status` draft → approved → posted, and back-fills
the metric columns weekly. John never edits it.

## What "good" looks like in 90 days

- A consistent 4-posts/week rhythm with zero missed weeks.
- Pinterest quietly compounding decor discovery.
- Fan-identity reels driving saves/shares and profile visits into the store.
- A library of process footage that never runs dry (the workshop is infinite content).
- The social audience feeding the same pixel the paid campaign optimizes on — the two engines
  reinforcing each other.
