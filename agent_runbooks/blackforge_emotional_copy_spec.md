# Blackforge Workshop — Emotional Copy Voice Spec (Dash-run rewrite)

**Job:** rewrite the remaining product descriptions to sell *emotion first, spec last*.
The Football Hoodie Desk Organizer and all 5 Sideline Cooler products are already live
in this voice — use them as the gold standard. Do NOT touch sabers (excluded by standing rule).

**Why (validated):** emotional copy converts ~2x rational-only. For merch like this the #1
trigger is **identity alignment** — copy that reflects who the buyer is or wants to be.
Share rate = belonging/identity; save rate = aspiration. Match the angle to the product.

---

## The formula (every listing)

**Hook (identity / feeling) → the object → one spec for the brain → CTA.**
First line is the hook — it's all most people read. Feeling first, specs underneath.
Keep the truthful, factual sections; just re-lead with emotion and add a gift/identity beat.

---

## Angle by category — assign the right one

| Product type | Lead angle | The feeling to hit |
|---|---|---|
| Fan gear (coolers, hoodie, uniform/crest) | **Identity / belonging** | "everyone knows whose side you're on" |
| Statues (Hanuman, Krishna, Maria, Shiva, Seraphim, Pope Leo, Mother & Child) | **Meaning + gift** | reverence, devotion, a meaningful gift |
| Lamps / pendants / lampshades | **Aspiration / pride-of-space** | "the room people notice — and you had it made" |
| Planters / vases | **Aspiration + gift** | a considered home; the easy, impressive gift |
| Desk / everyday organizers (pen/headphone/cable/controller holders, bins, boxes) | **Pride-of-space + gift**; gamer pieces (controller/skull/oni/kraken stands) → **identity/novelty** | "a desk that looks intentional" / "this is so me" |
| Clickers, dice tower, zombie duck, novelty | **Delight + gift** | "I have to show someone this" |

---

## Hard rules (do not break)

1. **Accuracy over hype.** Write from the product's photos and current copy. Never describe a
   form, color, or function the item doesn't have. Rigid prints are not "squishy," etc.
2. **Preserve the factual sections** already in each listing: any **Design Credit** line
   (e.g. "Designed by … with Commercial License Rights"), Made-to-order, personalization
   instructions, What-it-holds/fits, Ordering-several, Care. Re-lead with emotion; keep these.
3. **Customer language only** — no slicer/manufacturing jargon (infill, supports, vase mode,
   layer height, print-in-place). Say what it *is* and *does*.
4. **Only real colors/variants.** Don't invent options.
5. One clear CTA. Don't stuff hashtags or power-words to the point it reads fake.

## Process (batch by category)

For each product (skip anything tagged as a saber / product type Lightsaber):
1. `get-product` to read current copy + see the images.
2. Rewrite: new emotional hook (assigned angle) → keep the accurate object description →
   keep the factual sections → add one identity/gift beat → CTA.
3. `update-product` with the new `descriptionHtml`.
4. Work in batches by collection: Lamps → Planters → Vases → Statues → Desk/Everyday → Novelty.
   Report count done per batch.

## Gold-standard samples (match this level, adapt per item)

**Lamp (aspiration / pride-of-space):**
> Turn it on and the room has an opinion. The [shade] throws a warm, [texture] glow that makes
> a plain corner look like you meant it — the lamp guests ask about, and you get to say you had
> it made. Printed to order in [colors]. [spec/size]. Light up the room →

**Planter / vase (aspiration + gift):**
> Some plants deserve better than a plastic pot. [Name] gives [yours/theirs] a home with actual
> shape to it — the kind of piece that makes a shelf look considered. Easy win as a gift for the
> plant person who has every plant but no pot worth showing. [spec]. Make it theirs →

**Statue (meaning + gift):**
> A quiet presence for the shelf, the altar, the desk. [Name] is [accurate description], printed
> in [finish] — the kind of piece you give someone when it's supposed to mean something. [spec].

**Desk organizer (pride-of-space + gift):**
> Your desk called — it wants to look like you have your life together. [Name] keeps [what it
> holds] in one good-looking spot instead of a drawer. The small upgrade people notice. [spec].

**Novelty / clicker (delight + gift):**
> The little thing that makes people go "wait, what is that." [Name] is [accurate], and it's
> exactly the kind of pocket-money gift that lands better than it has any right to. [spec].
