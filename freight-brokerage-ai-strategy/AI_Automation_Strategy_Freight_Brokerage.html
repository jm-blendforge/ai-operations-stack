The Automation Layer — What Happens When a Load Goes At-Risk
Salesforce Flow orchestrates every exception from detection to resolution — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
Step 1 — Detection
MacroPoint or project44 detects a deviation — geofence miss, ETA slip, tracking gap, or route anomaly. The exception is pushed into the Revenova load record in real time via the pre-built integration.
Step 2 — Classification
A Salesforce Flow evaluates the exception against configurable rules — how late, which customer, which lane, what SLA — and classifies the load as On Time, At Risk, Late, or Critical. Load record updates automatically.
Step 3 — Alert
The assigned dispatcher receives a Salesforce notification. The account manager receives an Outlook alert. For Critical loads, an escalation task is created and assigned to a supervisor automatically.
Step 4 — Customer Notification
For At Risk and Late loads, a customer notification email is auto-drafted in Outlook using load data from Revenova. The rep reviews and sends in one click. For critical SLA accounts, it can fire automatically.
Step 5 — Performance Capture
Once a load delivers, on-time performance data is written to the carrier record in Salesforce. This feeds the carrier risk scoring model and OTP dashboard — turning every load into a data point that improves future decisions.
Runs 24/7 — No Human Required
Salesforce Flow runs continuously — nights, weekends, holidays. Exceptions surface automatically to the right person at the right moment, every time.
✓
Revenova advantage: Because MacroPoint, project44, TextLocate, and Trucker Tools all push data directly into Revenova load records via pre-built connectors, the Salesforce Flow automation layer reads live load data without any custom middleware. This is a configuration project, not a development project.

Work is sequenced to deliver stability and clarity first, then build the integration and automation layer, then activate AI and sales intelligence once the foundation is solid. Each phase has defined deliverables before the next begins.

Revenova + Salesforce Edition

Connected App Governance
As of September 2025, Salesforce requires all third-party integrations to be formally installed and admin-approved as Connected Apps. Every tool in this roadmap — ZoomInfo, E-Pay Manager, RingCentral, etc. — must be registered by the org admin before going live. This is a governance step, not a technical barrier.
Einstein AI — Data Threshold
